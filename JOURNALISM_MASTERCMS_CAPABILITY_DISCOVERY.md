- **INFERRED:** `thoidai.com.vn` is deployed on or generated by MasterCMS Ultimate Edition v2.9 (2026), based on its own rendered markers.
- **UNKNOWN:** MasterCMS repository path, private deployment path, runtime build details, database technology, internal hostname, reverse-proxy route, and production/staging topology.
